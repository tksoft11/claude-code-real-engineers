# The Book Factory: Styleguide

This styleguide is derived from the approved Chapter Prototype (Introduction). All agents MUST use this as the absolute reference for the Tone of Voice, formatting, and structural pacing for the rest of the book.

## 🗣️ Tone of Voice (น้ำเสียงการเล่าเรื่อง)
- **Friend Teaching Friend:** Act like a senior developer who is highly skilled but extremely humble and approachable. You are pulling up a chair to sit next to the reader.
- **Enthusiastic but Grounded:** Show excitement about AI's capabilities, but immediately ground it in reality (e.g., "AI is amazing, BUT if you don't control it, it explodes").
- **Language Level:** Thai language mixed with necessary English terms. However, English technical terms MUST be explained in plain Thai immediately upon first use.

## ✍️ Writing Mechanics (สไตล์การเขียน)
- **Paragraphs:** Keep them short. 3-4 sentences maximum. Use whitespace to let the reader breathe.
- **Emphasis:** Use bolding (`**text**`) for key concepts or punchlines to make skimming easy.
- **Transitions:** Use horizontal rules (`---`) to separate major sections.

## 🧠 Analogy Standards (มาตรฐานการอุปมาอุปไมย)
When using the Rule of Relatability, default to analogies involving:
1. **Office Dynamics** (e.g., The brilliant but forgetful intern).
2. **Everyday Analogies** (e.g., A scratchpad, giving directions to a taxi, a cooking recipe).
Avoid obscure sci-fi or overly complex engineering analogies.

## 🛑 What NOT to do (ข้อห้ามเด็ดขาด)
- Do NOT sound like a textbook or a Wikipedia article.
- Do NOT use phrases like "In conclusion" or "In summary". Use engaging subheaders like "🎯 ก้าวต่อไป (Action Item)".
- Do NOT skip the Hands-On section. Theory without practice is banned in this book.
